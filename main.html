— What's under the pack

Built to be serviced,
not replaced.

Every system on an AMPERE is modular. If a part wears out, it's swapped — not the whole bike.

A

Regenerative braking

Lever pressure feeds energy back into the pack — up to 18% more range in stop-start traffic.

B

Swappable battery

Pull the pack, drop in a charged one, ride again in under 90 seconds. No cable required.

C

Torque vectoring

The RS motor adjusts delivery mid-corner, putting power down exactly where the tire has grip.

D

Over-the-air tuning

Throttle map, regen strength, and ride modes update from the app — the bike gets better while it's parked.

The first bike that's made me stop thinking about the engine — because there isn't one to think about. It just goes.
